# Local Demo Sign-In

Run `node server.js` from this folder and open `http://localhost:4173`.

| Role | Email | Password | Demo data |
|---|---|---|---|
| Worker | `worker.demo@example.com` | `WorkerDemo!2026` | Areas V-01 through V-03 |
| Supervisor | `supervisor.demo@example.com` | `SupervisorDemo!2026` | Areas V-01 through V-06 |
| Admin | `admin.demo@example.com` | `AdminDemo!2026` | All synthetic households |

Household changes are saved in the current browser under the signed-in demo account. These credentials and role filters are client-side demonstration behavior, not real authentication or access control. Do not use real patient information.
